📜 Club Rules

  1. Members must maintain respectful behaviour towards other members and coordinators.
  2. Students should attend club meetings and activities regularly.
  3. College resources must be used responsibly.
  4. Members must follow the deadlines given for club events and activities.
  5. Any form of harassment, discrimination or misconduct is not permitted.
  6. Members should represent the college responsibly during external events.
  7. Students may participate in multiple clubs as long as their academic responsibilities are not affected.
